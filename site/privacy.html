Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026 · Terms of use

The short version
  • Perch has no analytics, no ads, no trackers and no crash-reporting SDKs — in the apps, the extension or this site.
  • Your feeds, reading and notes stay on your devices. We never see them.
  • We run no accounts: there is nothing to sign up for, and we hold no usernames, passwords or email addresses.
  • The only server we run is a relay for sync chains. It stores encrypted records it cannot read, and keeps no access logs.
  • We don't sell or share anything.
  • Perch is open source. Everything here can be checked in the code.

What this policy covers

Perch is open source: anyone can build it, change it and run their own server. The official services are a non-commercial project run by one person, Arif Çınar Tekin (“we”), as an individual. This policy covers only what he runs and publishes:

  • Our services: this site (perch.ws) and sync.perch.ws, the relay for sync chains. We are the data controller for these.
  • demo.perch.ws, a test server for App Review and anyone trying “Sync with a Perch Server”. Sign-up there is closed; it keeps the test account's library in plain text and may be reset at any time.
  • The apps we publish in the App Store, Google Play and the browser extension stores — that is, what they do on your device and which sites they contact.

It does not cover Perch Servers or sync relays run by other people, or builds of Perch published by others. If you connect to someone else's server (to sync your library, or to sign in for sharing notes), its operator is the data controller and their own policy applies. We have no access to those servers and can't see, change or delete anything on them. The apps show which server you're connected to, and its policy when it has published one.

Questions, requests and complaints: contact@perch.ws.

On your devices

The browser extension and the iPhone and Android apps work without any account. Your subscriptions, categories, read and starred articles, settings and notes are stored only on your device (in the browser's extension storage, or in the app's files on your phone, left out of cloud backups on Android). Nothing is sent to us.

To show you your feeds, your device itself connects to other sites:

  • The sites you subscribe to. Your device downloads each feed, and the full article when you open one, directly from the publisher. Like any visit, that site sees your IP address.
  • Images and videos inside articles load from wherever the publisher put them, without telling them which page you came from. Embedded YouTube and Vimeo players load from those services and are subject to their policies.
  • No favicons. Perch doesn't fetch site icons, which would tell each site that you follow it. Feeds get a coloured letter instead.

Some things never leave the device at all: the app lock's PIN (kept in the iPhone's Keychain, Android's Keystore or the browser), the fingerprint or face that opens it (checked by the phone, never seen by Perch), the camera picture when you scan a chain's QR code (read on the phone and not kept), the app icon and background picture you choose, the text size you read at, a page you share to Perch from another app, new-article notifications (made and shown by the device itself, with no push service; the extension asks for permission to show them only when you turn them on), and the starter packs and Explore list, which ship inside the apps, so choosing one tells no server anything.

Sharing a note “as text” hands it to your phone's share sheet or clipboard; where it goes from there is up to you. Public pages for notes exist only on Perch Servers that other people run.

The extension asks for no site access when installed. It asks for one site at a time when you add a feed or open the full text of an article from a new site, or for all sites only if you turn on automatic feed discovery. It looks at a page when you open Perch on it, or at each page as it loads if automatic discovery is on, and reads only the feed links the page names. If a page names none, it tries a few usual addresses on the same site (such as /feed), without cookies. None of this is sent to us.

Sync chains (sync.perch.ws)

A sync chain links your devices without an account. Everything is encrypted on your devices with a key derived from the chain code, which never leaves them. Even feed addresses and record names are hidden. Our relay stores:

  • a hash of the chain's access token (not the code, and not the key);
  • the encrypted records, each with an opaque slot name, a timestamp, its size, and whether it marks a deletion or read state that may be forgotten (not which item it is about);
  • when the chain was created and, roughly to the hour, when it was last used.

We can see how many records a chain has, how big they are and when they change. We cannot see what they contain, and nothing ties a chain to a person. Read, unstarred state is forgotten after 60 days, deleted items after 90 days, and a chain no device has used for 180 days is deleted with everything in it. You can delete a chain at any time from Settings → Sync.

If someone took our server

We keep as little as possible, so that a stolen disk or a leaked backup says nothing about you. The relay holds only encrypted chain records: without your chain code they can't be read, and there are no accounts, names, email addresses or IP addresses to link them to anyone. The relay also sends no code to your devices: the apps come from the stores, so a compromised relay can't take your chain code. At worst it could stop passing records on.

Deleted data is overwritten in the database rather than left on disk, and is gone from backups within 14 days. How to check which code our server runs is on the transparency page.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, usernames, passwords or email addresses.
  • No analytics, telemetry, usage statistics or A/B tests.
  • No advertising, ad identifiers or tracking pixels.
  • No third-party SDKs for crash reporting, marketing or anything else.
  • No access logs: the relay writes no record of requests.
  • No stored IP addresses. Your device's address reaches the relay, as with any connection, but it isn't written anywhere.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera recordings or microphone.
  • We don't sell or rent data, don't share it with advertisers or data brokers, and don't use it to train AI models.

Cookies and local storage

We set no cookies anywhere, so there is no cookie banner. This site remembers your language choice in your browser's local storage, which never leaves your device.

Who else is involved

  • OVHcloud hosts the relay (sync.perch.ws) and the demo server. It is currently in Canada; we plan to move it to the EU. Encrypted chain records are stored there.
  • Cloudflare provides DNS for perch.ws and hosts this site. Traffic to sync.perch.ws does not pass through Cloudflare.
  • GitHub hosts Perch's code and its Discussions, where people ask and suggest things in the open. Anything you post there is under GitHub's terms and privacy statement, as its controller.
  • Apple, Google, Microsoft and Mozilla distribute the apps through their stores under their own policies. If you choose to share analytics with app developers on your iPhone, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. The Android app contains no analytics or crash reporting of its own; Google Play may show us aggregate install and crash counts.

Using sync chains through our relay therefore means encrypted records are transferred outside Türkiye and the EU. If you don't want that, use Perch without a chain, or run your own relay.

How long we keep things

  • Chains: as described above (60 / 90 / 180 days), or until you delete them.
  • Backups: the relay keeps daily database backups for 14 days. Something deleted is gone from the backups within 14 days.

Deleting your data

Everything that's yours is on your devices: remove it in the app, or uninstall it. To remove a sync chain from our relay, use Settings → Sync → Delete the chain on any device in it. You can export your subscriptions as OPML at any time.

Your rights

Under Türkiye's data protection law (KVKK, article 11) and, where it applies, the EU GDPR, you can ask what we hold about you, get a copy, have it corrected or deleted, and object to how it is used. We hold nothing that identifies you, but you can always write to contact@perch.ws. We answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or your local data protection authority.

The legal basis for what the relay stores is that it's needed to provide the service you asked for (KVKK art. 5(2)(c); GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).

Changes

If we change what we collect, we'll update this page first and announce it here and in the apps. Every version of this page is in the project's history.

Cihazlarında

Tarayıcı eklentisi ile iPhone ve Android uygulamaları hesap olmadan çalışır. Abonelikler, kategoriler, okunan ve yıldızlanan makaleler, ayarlar ve notlar yalnızca cihazında saklanır (tarayıcının eklenti deposunda ya da telefonunda uygulamanın dosyalarında; Android'de bulut yedeğine dahil edilmez). Bize hiçbir şey gönderilmez.

Feed'lerini gösterebilmek için cihazın başka sitelere doğrudan bağlanır:

  • Abone olduğun siteler. Cihazın her feed'i ve açtığın makalenin tamamını doğrudan yayıncıdan indirir. Her ziyarette olduğu gibi o site IP adresini görür.
  • Makalelerdeki resim ve videolar, yayıncı nereye koyduysa oradan, hangi sayfadan geldiğin söylenmeden yüklenir. Gömülü YouTube ve Vimeo oynatıcıları bu hizmetlerden yüklenir ve onların politikalarına tabidir.
  • Site simgesi yok. Perch site simgelerini indirmez; indirse her site onu takip ettiğini öğrenirdi. Feed'ler bunun yerine renkli bir harf alır.

Bazı şeyler cihazdan hiç çıkmaz: uygulama kilidinin PIN'i (iPhone'un Keychain'inde, Android'in Keystore'unda ya da tarayıcıda), kilidi açan parmak izi ya da yüz (telefon kontrol eder, Perch hiç görmez), bir zincirin QR kodunu okuturken kameranın görüntüsü (telefonda okunur, saklanmaz), seçtiğin uygulama ikonu ve arka plan resmi, okuduğun yazı boyutu, başka bir uygulamadan Perch'e paylaştığın sayfa, yeni makale bildirimleri (bir bildirim servisi olmadan cihazın kendisi oluşturup gösterir; eklenti bildirim iznini yalnızca sen açtığında ister) ve uygulamaların içinde gelen başlangıç paketleri ile Keşfet listesi; birini seçmek hiçbir sunucuya bir şey söylemez.

Bir notu “metin olarak” paylaşmak onu telefonunun paylaş menüsüne ya da panoya verir; oradan nereye gideceği sana kalmıştır. Notlar için herkese açık sayfalar yalnızca başkalarının çalıştırdığı Perch sunucularında vardır.

Eklenti kurulurken hiçbir siteye erişim istemez. Ondan bir feed eklediğinde ya da yeni bir sitedeki makalenin tamamını açtığında yalnızca o siteye, otomatik feed bulmayı açarsan tüm sitelere erişim ister. Bir sayfaya orada Perch'ü açtığında, ya da otomatik bulma açıksa her sayfa yüklendiğinde bakar ve yalnızca sayfanın belirttiği feed bağlantılarını okur. Sayfa hiç belirtmiyorsa aynı sitede birkaç bilinen adresi (/feed gibi) çerezsiz dener. Bunların hiçbiri bize gönderilmez.

Senkron zincirleri (sync.perch.ws)

Bir senkron zinciri cihazlarını hesapsız olarak birbirine bağlar. Her şey cihazlarında, zincir kodundan türetilen ve cihazlardan hiç çıkmayan bir anahtarla şifrelenir. Feed adresleri ve kayıt adları bile gizlidir. Aktarıcımız şunları saklar:

  • zincirin erişim anahtarının bir özeti (kodun kendisi ya da şifreleme anahtarı değil);
  • şifreli kayıtlar; her biri anlamsız bir yuva adı, bir zaman damgası, boyutu ve bir silme mi, yoksa unutulabilecek bir okundu bilgisi mi olduğuyla (hangi öğeye ait olduğu değil);
  • zincirin ne zaman oluşturulduğu ve saat düzeyinde, en son ne zaman kullanıldığı.

Bir zincirde kaç kayıt olduğunu, ne büyüklükte olduklarını ve ne zaman değiştiklerini görebiliriz; içlerinde ne olduğunu göremeyiz, ve hiçbir şey bir zinciri bir kişiye bağlamaz. Okunmuş ve yıldızsız öğelerin bilgisi 60 gün, silinen öğeler 90 gün sonra unutulur; 180 gündür hiçbir cihazın kullanmadığı bir zincir içindekilerle birlikte silinir. Bir zinciri istediğin zaman Ayarlar → Senkron'dan silebilirsin.

Sunucumuz ele geçirilirse

Çalınan bir diskin ya da sızan bir yedeğin senin hakkında hiçbir şey söylememesi için olabildiğince az şey tutarız. Aktarıcıda yalnızca şifreli zincir kayıtları vardır: zincir kodun olmadan okunamazlar, ve onları birine bağlayacak hesap, ad, e-posta ya da IP adresi yoktur. Aktarıcı cihazlarına kod da göndermez: uygulamalar mağazalardan gelir, bu yüzden ele geçirilmiş bir aktarıcı zincir kodunu alamaz. En kötü durumda kayıtları iletmeyi bırakabilir.

Silinen veriler diskte bırakılmaz, veritabanında üzerine yazılır ve 14 gün içinde yedeklerden de çıkar. Sunucumuzun hangi kodu çalıştırdığını nasıl kontrol edeceğin şeffaflık sayfasında.
