Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026 · Terms of use

The short version
  • Perch has no analytics, no ads, no trackers and no crash-reporting SDKs — in the apps, the extension, the web app or this site.
  • Without an account, your feeds, reading and notes stay on your device. We never see them.
  • Sync chains are end-to-end encrypted. Our relay stores data it cannot read.
  • If you make an account on app.perch.ws, the server stores your library so it can sync it — not end-to-end encrypted. Details below.
  • We don't keep access logs or store IP addresses. We don't sell or share anything.
  • Perch is open source. Everything here can be checked in the code.

What this policy covers

Perch is open source: anyone can build it, change it and run their own server. This policy covers only what [[OPERATOR]] (“we”) runs and publishes:

  • Our services: perch.ws, app.perch.ws and sync.perch.ws. We are the data controller for these.
  • The apps we publish in the App Store and the browser extension stores — that is, what they do on your device and which sites they contact.

It does not cover Perch Servers or sync relays run by other people, or builds of Perch published by others. If you sign in to someone else's server, its operator is the data controller and their own policy applies. We have no access to those servers and can't see, change or delete anything on them. The apps show which server you're connected to, and that server's own policy when it has published one.

Questions, requests and complaints about our services: [[CONTACT]].

Using Perch without an account

The browser extension and the iPhone app work without any account. Your subscriptions, categories, read and starred articles, settings and notes are stored only on your device (in the browser's extension storage, or in the app's files on your iPhone). Nothing is sent to us.

To show you your feeds, your device itself connects to other sites:

  • The sites you subscribe to. Your device downloads each feed, and the full article when you open one, directly from the publisher. Like any visit, that site sees your IP address.
  • Images and videos inside articles load from wherever the publisher put them, without telling them which page you came from. Embedded YouTube and Vimeo players load from those services and are subject to their policies.
  • No favicons. Perch doesn't fetch site icons, which would tell each site that you follow it. Feeds get a coloured letter instead.

The extension asks for no site access when installed. It asks for one site at a time when you add a feed from it, or for all sites only if you turn on automatic feed discovery. It reads a page only when you open Perch on it, and only the feed links the page names.

Sync chains (sync.perch.ws)

A sync chain links your devices without an account. Everything is encrypted on your devices with a key derived from the chain code, which never leaves them. Even feed addresses and record names are hidden. Our relay stores:

  • a hash of the chain's access token (not the code, and not the key);
  • the encrypted records, each with an opaque slot name, a timestamp and its size;
  • when the chain was created and, roughly to the hour, when it was last used.

We can see how many records a chain has, how big they are and when they change. We cannot see what they contain. Read state is forgotten after 60 days, deleted items after 90 days, and a chain no device has used for 180 days is deleted with everything in it. You can delete a chain at any time from Settings → Sync.

A Perch account on app.perch.ws

An account lets you read on the web and sync through our server. To do that, the server needs your library in a form it can use. That means it is stored in plain text on our server, not end-to-end encrypted. If that's not what you want, use a sync chain or no sync at all. We don't look at your data except when needed to run or fix the service, or when the law requires it.

Our server fetches the feeds you follow, so publishers see our server, not you. Feeds and articles are stored once and shared by everyone who follows them; full article text is cached the same way, with no record of who asked for it.

Shared notes

Notes are private. When you share one, Perch publishes a copy at a random address (app.perch.ws/shared/…) that anyone with the link can read. The page shows your note, the article's title, link and source, your @username and the dates. It asks search engines not to index it, but anyone who has the link can copy it. Editing the note updates the page; deleting the note or choosing “Stop sharing” removes it.

Each shared page has a report form. A report stores the reason, what you write and, only if you give it, a way to reach you. We use reports only to review the page.

Email

We send email only when you ask for something: a sign-up or password-reset code, a code to confirm a new address, or a notice that an address already has an account. Codes are stored only as a keyed hash and expire after 10 minutes. Mail is delivered by Resend from mail.perch.ws. Servers run by other people never use our email service.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics, telemetry, usage statistics or A/B tests.
  • No advertising, ad identifiers or tracking pixels.
  • No third-party SDKs for crash reporting, marketing or anything else.
  • No access logs: the web server writes no record of requests.
  • No stored IP addresses. To stop abuse (password guessing, sign-up floods), the server counts recent requests per IP address in memory. Those counters vanish within an hour, or when the server restarts, and are never written to disk.
  • No reading history beyond the read and starred flags above.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We don't sell or rent data, don't share it with advertisers or data brokers, and don't use it to train AI models.

The server's own output contains start-up messages and error messages (for example “sending email failed”); these don't include IP addresses or what you read.

Cookies and local storage

app.perch.ws sets one cookie: your session, so you stay signed in. It's HTTP-only and sent only to app.perch.ws. There are no other cookies, so there is no cookie banner. This site (perch.ws) sets no cookies; it remembers your language choice in your browser's local storage, which never leaves your device.

Who else is involved

  • OVHcloud hosts our server (app.perch.ws and sync.perch.ws). It is currently in Canada; we plan to move it to the EU. Your account data and encrypted chain records are stored there.
  • Resend (USA) delivers our emails, so it processes your email address and the message.
  • Cloudflare provides DNS for perch.ws and hosts this site. Traffic to app.perch.ws and sync.perch.ws does not pass through Cloudflare.
  • Apple, Google, Microsoft and Mozilla distribute the apps through their stores under their own policies. If you choose to share analytics with app developers on your iPhone, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports.

Using our servers therefore means your data is transferred outside Türkiye and the EU. If you don't want that, use Perch without an account, or run your own server.

How long we keep things

  • Account data: until you delete it or the account.
  • Email codes: 10 minutes. Sessions: 180 days after last use.
  • Chains: as described above (60 / 90 / 180 days).
  • Backups: the server keeps daily database backups for 14 days. Something you delete is gone from the backups within 14 days.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account yourself, in the web app (Settings → Delete account), the iPhone app (Settings → Delete account) or the extension (Settings → Sync). It deletes your account, sessions, library, read state, settings, notes and shared pages at once. Data on your devices stays there until you remove it or uninstall the app. You can export your subscriptions as OPML at any time.

Your rights

Under Türkiye's data protection law (KVKK, article 11) and, where it applies, the EU GDPR, you can ask what we hold about you, get a copy, have it corrected or deleted, and object to how it is used. Most of this you can do yourself in the app; for anything else write to [[CONTACT]]. We answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or your local data protection authority.

The legal basis for what we store is that it's needed to provide the service you asked for (KVKK art. 5(2)(c); GDPR art. 6(1)(b)), and, for abuse protection, our legitimate interest in keeping the service working.

Children

Perch accounts are for people 13 and older.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we'll update this page first and, for anything significant, tell account holders by email. Every version of this page is in the project's history.

Perch'ü hesapsız kullanmak

Tarayıcı eklentisi ve iPhone uygulaması hesap olmadan çalışır. Abonelikler, kategoriler, okunan ve yıldızlanan makaleler, ayarlar ve notlar yalnızca cihazında saklanır (tarayıcının eklenti deposunda ya da iPhone'da uygulamanın dosyalarında). Bize hiçbir şey gönderilmez.

Akışları gösterebilmek için cihazın başka sitelere doğrudan bağlanır:

  • Abone olduğun siteler. Cihazın her akışı ve açtığın makalenin tam metnini doğrudan yayıncıdan indirir. Her ziyarette olduğu gibi o site IP adresini görür.
  • Makalelerdeki resim ve videolar yayıncının koyduğu yerden, hangi sayfadan geldiğin söylenmeden yüklenir. Gömülü YouTube ve Vimeo oynatıcıları o hizmetlerden yüklenir ve onların politikalarına tabidir.
  • Site simgesi indirilmez. Simgeleri indirmek her siteye onu takip ettiğini söylerdi. Bunun yerine akışlar renkli bir harfle gösterilir.

Eklenti kurulurken hiçbir siteye erişim istemez. Ondan bir akış eklediğinde yalnızca o site için izin ister; tüm siteler için izni yalnızca otomatik akış bulmayı açarsan ister. Bir sayfayı yalnızca Perch'ü o sayfada açtığında okur, o da sayfanın belirttiği akış bağlantılarıyla sınırlıdır.

Senkron zincirleri (sync.perch.ws)

Senkron zinciri, cihazlarını hesap olmadan birbirine bağlar. Her şey, zincir kodundan türetilen ve cihazlarından hiç çıkmayan bir anahtarla cihazlarında şifrelenir. Akış adresleri ve kayıt adları bile gizlidir. Aktarıcımız şunları saklar:

  • zincirin erişim belirtecinin özeti (kodun ya da anahtarın kendisi değil);
  • şifreli kayıtlar: her biri anlamsız bir yuva adı, bir zaman damgası ve boyutuyla;
  • zincirin ne zaman oluşturulduğu ve saat hassasiyetinde en son ne zaman kullanıldığı.

Bir zincirde kaç kayıt olduğunu, ne kadar büyük olduklarını ve ne zaman değiştiklerini görebiliriz; içlerinde ne olduğunu göremeyiz. Okundu bilgisi 60 gün, silinen öğeler 90 gün sonra unutulur. 180 gün boyunca hiçbir cihazın kullanmadığı zincir, içindekilerle birlikte silinir. Bir zinciri istediğin an Ayarlar → Senkron'dan silebilirsin.

app.perch.ws'te bir Perch hesabı

Hesap; web'de okumanı ve sunucumuz üzerinden senkronlamanı sağlar. Bunun için sunucunun kitaplığını kullanabileceği bir biçimde tutması gerekir; bu yüzden kitaplığın sunucumuzda düz metin olarak saklanır, uçtan uca şifreli değildir. Bunu istemiyorsan senkron zinciri kullan ya da hiç senkronlama. Verine; hizmeti çalıştırmak ya da bir sorunu gidermek gerekmedikçe veya yasa zorunlu kılmadıkça bakmayız.
