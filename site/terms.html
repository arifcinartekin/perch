2. Your account

  • You must be 13 or older to make an account.
  • One account per person.
  • Keep your password to yourself, and keep your recovery code somewhere safe. Only a key derived from your password reaches us, and only a hash of the recovery code, so we can't tell you either. If you lose both, we can't recover the account.
  • Sync chains have no account: whoever has the chain code can read and change its data. If every device loses the code, we can't recover it — we never had the key.

3. Your notes

Your notes are yours. You give us only the permission we need to store them, sync them to your devices and, when you share one, show it to anyone with the link — for as long as it is shared. Stop sharing or delete the note and the permission ends.

You are responsible for what you share. Quote articles briefly and link to the original; don't paste whole articles or other people's work.

4. What's not allowed

If you break these rules we may hide shared pages, and for serious or repeated breaches suspend or delete the account. Where we can, we'll tell you why and give you a chance to respond.

5. Reporting content and taking it down

Every shared note has a Report form at the bottom. You can also write to [[CONTACT]] with the page's address and what's wrong with it.

Under Türkiye's Law No. 5651, we act as a hosting provider for shared notes: we don't check content before it's published, but when we're notified of unlawful content we review it and remove it if it's unlawful. We aim to review reports within 48 hours, and we comply with orders from courts and competent authorities within the time the law sets. A removed page says that it was removed. If your page was removed and you think that's wrong, write to us and we'll look again.

Perch is free and provided “as is”. We work to keep it running and your data safe, but we can't promise it will always be available, error-free or never lose data. Keep your own copy of what matters: you can export your subscriptions as OPML at any time.

We may change or stop features. If we ever shut down a service that holds your data, we'll announce it on perch.ws and in the apps at least 30 days ahead, so you can take your data with you. We don't have your email address, so we can't write to you.
